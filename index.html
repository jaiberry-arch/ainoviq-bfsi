Before you pitch

Claims guardrails

  • Quote the numbers in this brief only as indicative POC targets, measured against a control group and agreed up front. Never as results or guarantees.
  • Describe the collections agent built with a loan recovery agency as an ongoing collaboration, not a proven deployment, and attribute no results to it.
  • Make no compliance claims until they are verified.
  • Pitch outbound campaigns only. Never offer inbound calling, a helpline, customer service or query resolution.
  • Label sample conversations and the reason breakdown as illustrative.

What a POC needs from the prospect

  • A sponsor, and one use case and segment to start with
  • Their SOP, sample calls and a test list
  • Access to their CRM, or a file drop, for writing outcomes back
  • A named contact who refreshes the data during the pilot

A POC runs on 2,000–5,000 customers against a control group. Missing call data is the most common blocker, so agree it first.

2 · All BFSI use cases

Three AI agents. One customer journey.

Acquire, activate, collect. Win the customer faster, set them up right from day one, and collect on time. A prospect can start with one and add the others on the same platform.

One customer, one context, end to end: what the lead call learns shapes the welcome call, and an e-NACH set up at onboarding prevents a bounce later.

Scope

We run outbound campaigns on a list the customer sends us. We do not offer customer service, query resolution or an inbound helpline, and we never answer calls customers make to the bank. Every use case here starts from a trigger list and a dialled call.
